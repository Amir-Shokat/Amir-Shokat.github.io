A bit about me

Where I'm from

I grew up in Birjand, a dry city in eastern Iran, and studied Financial Management at Kharazmi University. That's where finance clicked for me: markets are full of patterns that look obvious and turn out to be wrong, and I like finding out why.

Why the Netherlands

I came to Utrecht in 2023 for a master's in Banking & Finance, and the country won me over: the canals, the directness, the bikes, and how even a rainy afternoon can be gezellig. I'm learning Dutch langzaam maar zeker, slowly but surely.
