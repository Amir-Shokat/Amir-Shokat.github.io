Hoi, I'm Amir.

I work in finance, and I genuinely enjoy it, which surprises people at parties. My days are about measuring risk, cleaning up messy data and asking the slightly annoying question: "are we sure this number means what we think it means?"

Where I'm from

I grew up in Iran and studied Financial Management at Kharazmi University. That's where finance clicked for me: markets are full of patterns that look obvious and turn out to be wrong, and I like finding out why.

Why the Netherlands

I came to Utrecht in 2023 for a master's in Banking & Finance, and the country won me over: the canals, the directness, the bikes, and how even a rainy afternoon can be gezellig. I'm learning Dutch langzaam maar zeker, slowly but surely.
