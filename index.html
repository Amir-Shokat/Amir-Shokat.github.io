Hoi! I'm Amir,
a finance person in Utrecht.

I like markets the way some people like puzzles: there is always a pattern that looks obvious, and it is almost always wrong. I spend my days measuring risk, cleaning messy data and checking whether the numbers actually mean what we think they mean.

Where I'm from

I grew up in Iran, where I studied Financial Management at Kharazmi University and fell for finance properly: first through investment courses, then through the endless spreadsheets that come with them. I still think a well-built spreadsheet is a small work of art.

Why the Netherlands

In 2023 I came to Utrecht for a master's in Banking & Finance, and the Netherlands has kept me. I love the canals, the directness, the bikes everywhere and how a grey afternoon can still be gezellig. I'm learning Dutch: langzaam maar zeker, slowly but surely, and mostly by pretending to understand the jokes.

What I do

Most recently I owned the portfolio and return calculations behind a trading app used by millions, and before that I monitored market risk and reconciled trades at an investment bank. On the research side, I work on climate risk in asset prices. More on the CV and research pages.

Outside of finance

You'll find me swimming, on a hiking trail or playing music, usually not all at once. I also write the occasional post about things I find interesting: interests & posts.
