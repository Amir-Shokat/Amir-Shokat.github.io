About

I work on market risk and portfolio data. At Damavand Investment Bank I monitored the risk of equity, fixed-income and gold funds (VaR, stress tests, liquidity) and automated the daily trade reconciliation with Python and SQL. Most recently, at Mofid Securities, I was the product owner for the return figures in Easy Trader, an investing app with more than 7 million users.

On the research side, my MSc thesis at Utrecht asked whether unusual temperatures show up in US stock prices. I later rebuilt it from scratch, and it is still the question I'm working on.

Where I'm from

I grew up in Birjand, a dry city in eastern Iran, and studied Financial Management at Kharazmi University. That's where finance clicked for me: markets are full of patterns that look obvious and turn out to be wrong, and I like finding out why.

Why the Netherlands

I came to Utrecht in 2023 for a master's in Banking & Finance, and the country won me over: the canals, the directness, the bikes, and how even a rainy afternoon can be gezellig. I'm learning Dutch langzaam maar zeker, slowly but surely.
