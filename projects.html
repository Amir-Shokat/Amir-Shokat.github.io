Projects

Each project started as a thesis, coursework or work analysis. I rebuilt them so anyone can run them, fixed the errors I found in the originals, and wrote down what I learned. The software packages come with unit tests.

MSc thesis · Utrecht University

Warming Trends: Replication & Audit

A reproducible rebuild of my thesis: firm-level returns and volatility for about 18,000 US firms matched to PRISM temperature anomalies at each headquarters, with fixed-effects regressions and two-way clustered errors.

Found: the original matched weather to firms by city name only, so 45% of firm-months got another town's weather. Once that is corrected, there is no detectable temperature effect.

Utrecht University · Risk Management

Yield-Curve PCA & Interest-Rate VaR

PCA of daily euro yield-curve moves and VaR for a swap portfolio, comparing 1, 2 and 3-factor models with historical simulation, on public ECB data.

Found: one bad day of quotes had created the "third factor" in the original assignment. A two-factor model understates VaR by about 20%.

Utrecht University · FinTech

Is Bitcoin the New Gold?

Regressions of Bitcoin and gold returns and volatility on the Caldara-Iacoviello Geopolitical Risk index, with macro controls, HAC errors and robustness checks. Fully reproducible from public data.

Found: Bitcoin is no geopolitical hedge; gold reacts only with a one-day lag.

Utrecht University · Text Mining

News Sentiment & Media Bias

Did CNBC cover Donald Trump in a different tone? Article and sentence-level sentiment, significance tests, bootstrap intervals and TF-IDF similarity, for any term and any news CSV.

Found: no difference in tone (p = 0.94), but slightly less subjective language (p = 0.02).
