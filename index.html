MSc thesis · Utrecht University

Warming Trends: Replication & Audit

A reproducible rebuild of my thesis: firm-level returns and volatility for about 18,000 US firms matched to PRISM temperature anomalies at each headquarters, with fixed-effects regressions and two-way clustered errors.

Found: the original matched weather to firms by city name only, so 45% of firm-months got another town's weather. Once that is corrected, there is no detectable temperature effect.

MSc thesis · Utrecht University · 2024

Warming Trends: Analysing the Impact of Temperature Anomaly on the Risk and Return of the US Stock Market

A panel of climate data, firm-level returns and company characteristics for about 18,000 US-listed firms. Fixed-effects panel regressions test whether temperature anomalies are priced into equity risk and returns. In a later replication and audit I rebuilt the pipeline, corrected how weather was matched to headquarters, and found no detectable effect.
