About

What interests me most is financial economics: using economic theory and econometrics to understand how people behave in markets, and how that shows up in prices.

My research is on climate risk in asset prices. My MSc thesis at Utrecht asked whether unusually hot or cold months at a firm's headquarters move US stock returns. I later rebuilt it from scratch and, with the data corrected, found no effect. My ideas now are about where to look instead: heat at the plants where firms operate versus heat where their investors live, and whether what firms say about climate risk still means anything once AI makes it cheap to write.
